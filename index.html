The Goldsmiths of Mount Lebanon & Beirut

For millennia, Lebanon has stood at the crossroads of civilization, celebrated across the Mediterranean for exquisite goldsmithing, intricate filigree, and flawless diamond setting.

From the ancient Phoenician maritime artisans of Byblos who traded noble gold across the seas, to the storied jewelry quarter of Beirut’s Bab Idriss and Souk El Sagha, Maison Aurum honors this eternal heritage. Every solitaire, wedding set, and Cedar medallion is conceived and hand-set in our Lebanese ateliers using certified conflict-free diamonds and pure 18KT gold.
